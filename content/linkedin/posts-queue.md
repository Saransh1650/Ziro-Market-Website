### 2 — Central banks and gold
Central banks bought 2,175 tonnes of gold last year, a record. This isn't about jewellery.

After the West froze Russia's dollar reserves in 2022, every non-Western central bank learned one lesson: dollars held abroad can be frozen. Gold in your own vault cannot.

China, Russia, India (the RBI), Turkey, Poland, all quietly swapping dollars for gold.

It's one of the biggest reasons gold hit an all-time high.

Not investment advice.

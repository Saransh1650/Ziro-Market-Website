Cheaper oil did what strong domestic data could not. **The Sensex jumped 443.97 points or 0.58% to 76,922.64 on July 2, 2026, and the Nifty 50 rose 0.59% to reclaim the 24,000 mark at 24,005.85, as crude oil slid to a four-month low and domestic funds kept buying.** The rebound snapped a two-session losing streak and put the psychological levels back within reach.

## What Happened

The rebound was led by cheaper crude. **Brent crude fell toward a four-month low near $71 as US-Iran peace talks in Qatar showed steady progress, easing the supply fears that had gripped the market for weeks.** For India, which imports most of its oil, falling crude is a direct tailwind, lowering the import bill and inflation pressure.

Domestic buyers did the rest. Local mutual funds kept pouring money into large-caps, absorbing mild foreign fund outflows, and the combination pushed the Nifty back above 24,000. Eternal Limited rose 3.97%, Nestle India 3.40%, and Asian Paints 3.01%, leading the gains.

The exception was technology. **IT stocks fell as US tech shares dropped overnight and global semiconductor stocks tumbled on growth data**, with HCL Tech down 3.43%, Tech Mahindra 3.18%, and TCS 2.49%. The sector's weakness capped the rally but did not derail it.

## Why This Matters for Investors

The session showed how central oil has become to the market's direction. **With crude at a four-month low, the biggest overhang on an oil-importing economy is easing, and the market rewarded it directly.** The trade-deal hopes and strong domestic data mattered, but it was the oil move that flipped sentiment back to risk-on.

The return of domestic buying is just as important. Local institutions absorbing foreign selling shows a domestic cushion that has repeatedly supported the market in 2026, reducing its dependence on foreign flows for direction.

For long-term investors, the split between leading consumption stocks and lagging IT is worth noting. It reflects a market leaning into the domestic growth story while staying wary of global tech demand, a theme likely to persist into the Q1 FY27 earnings season.

## Market Reaction

Breadth was positive, led by consumption and rate-sensitive names, with only IT as a notable drag. **The leadership of domestic-facing stocks over export-linked IT underlines that the rally was built on India's own tailwinds, chiefly cheaper oil, rather than global cues.**

Reclaiming 24,000 is the key technical marker. After slipping below it earlier in the week, the Nifty has now recovered the level, which traders will watch to see whether it can hold as the month's big events approach.

## What Investors Should Watch

The first thing to watch is crude oil. **Since the rebound leaned on oil hitting a four-month low, the path of crude, tied to the US-Iran talks in Qatar, will be the key swing factor.** Any reversal higher would pressure the market and the rupee.

The second is the July calendar. The July 24 India-US trade deadline and the Q1 FY27 earnings season, starting mid-month with the IT majors, are the next big tests, as laid out in our [stock market in July 2026](/blog/stock-market-july-2026-what-to-watch) guide.

The third is foreign flows. Domestic buying has cushioned the market, but a sustained return of foreign buyers would add firepower, so watch whether the mild outflows reverse.

## Risks to Monitor

The clearest risk is an oil rebound. If the US-Iran talks stall and crude climbs, the main support behind this rally would weaken, hurting stocks and the rupee.

A second risk is the IT sector. With global tech sentiment soft and the Q1 earnings season near, weak guidance from the IT majors could weigh on a heavyweight part of the index.

The third is the trade deal. A miss on the July 24 deadline would let the temporary US tariff snap back, denting the optimism supporting exporters. This is general information, not investment advice.

Reclaiming 24,000 on the back of cheap oil is an encouraging start to July, but the month's real tests, the trade deal and earnings, are still ahead. For now, the market has found its footing where it lost it, at the 24,000 line.

## What Is Happening

Bitcoin has gone from reclaiming support to defending it. **After jumping back above $60,000 last week, the price has settled around $62,500, easing slightly on July 6 as traders banked some of the gain**, but holding the level that matters most. Ethereum has been the stronger performer, up more than 13% over the week to around $1,766.

The catalyst came from the US economy. **June's jobs report showed just 57,000 jobs added, well below expectations, with unemployment ticking up to 4.2%**, a softer reading that revived hopes the Federal Reserve will cut interest rates to support growth. For crypto, which trades like a high-risk version of the stock market, the prospect of cheaper money is a powerful tailwind.

The reaction shows how tightly crypto is now tied to macro. The same rate expectations that pressured Bitcoin when the Fed was hawkish are now supporting it, and this week's small pullback is the market pausing to see whether those rate-cut hopes are confirmed by the next round of data.

## Why This Matters

Holding $60,000 matters as much as reclaiming it did. **Defending a level it had just recovered suggests the earlier break below was a shakeout rather than the start of a deeper fall**, at least for now, with buyers still willing to step in on dips.

The macro backdrop is the key driver. Crypto tends to do best when liquidity is loose and rates are falling, so any data that pushes the Fed toward cuts, like last week's weak jobs report, tends to lift Bitcoin. The market is now parsing every US data point for confirmation of that shift.

For Indian investors, the swings come with a tax sting. India taxes crypto gains at an effective 34%, with a 1% TDS on transactions and no loss offset against other income, one of the harshest regimes globally. That makes timing and risk management especially costly to get wrong for Indian crypto holders.

## What To Watch

The first thing to watch is US data and the Fed. **Because this bounce was built on rate-cut hopes, the next jobs and inflation reports, and the Fed's response, will decide whether the recovery extends or fades.**

The second is whether Bitcoin keeps holding above $60,000. Staying above the level would confirm the reclaim, while a slip back below it would suggest the bounce was fragile.

The third is whether ETF and institutional flows return. The 2025 rally was powered by inflows, so a pickup in buying from large funds would be an early sign that appetite is coming back in force.

## Risks To Monitor

The clearest risk is that the rate-cut hopes fade. If later data comes in stronger, or the Fed pushes back, the macro tailwind behind this bounce could reverse quickly.

A second risk is that the recovery stalls near resistance. Bitcoin has a long way back to its highs, and rallies during a broader downtrend can run out of steam.

The third is the broader macro backdrop. A stronger dollar or renewed risk-off sentiment would pressure crypto again, since it feels macro shifts most acutely. This is general information, not investment advice.

Bitcoin holding above $62,000 is an encouraging follow-through after last week's bounce, but it still rests entirely on how the US economy and the Fed unfold from here. In a market this macro-driven, the next data point could just as easily take back the gain.

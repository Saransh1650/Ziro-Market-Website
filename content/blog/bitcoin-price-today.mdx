Crypto has shaken off the war scare. **Bitcoin has recovered to about $64,000 as of July 10, 2026, up around 2.8% over the past week, with Ethereum near $1,795 and up about 2.7%, as risk appetite returned and the mid-week geopolitical fright faded.** After slipping when the US-Iran conflict flared, crypto has climbed back in step with a rebounding stock market.

The move fits crypto's pattern: it falls fastest when fear spikes, then recovers quickly once the mood calms. This week delivered both halves of that cycle.

## What Is Happening

Bitcoin has round-tripped the week's fear. **After slipping toward $62,800 when the US-Iran strikes rattled markets, it has recovered to about $64,000 as calm returned**, with Ethereum climbing back to around $1,795. Both are up over the week, a swing that mirrors the crash-and-rebound seen in equities.

The driver was risk sentiment. When the conflict escalated and oil spiked mid-week, investors fled risk assets and crypto fell with them. **As oil eased, the ceasefire held despite the strain, and stocks bounced, the same risk-on mood lifted crypto**, which trades as one of the market's highest-beta assets.

Underneath the geopolitics, the rate story still helps. Hopes that soft US data will push the Federal Reserve toward interest rate cuts remain a background tailwind for crypto, and with the immediate scare fading, that optimism has room to reassert itself.

## Why This Matters

The quick recovery shows crypto's resilience and its risk. **Bouncing back within days of a geopolitical shock suggests buyers still see dips as opportunities**, but the sharp fall that preceded it is a reminder that crypto drops hard when broad fear hits. It is a two-way street, and both directions can be fast.

The macro backdrop remains the key driver. Crypto tends to do best when liquidity is loose and risk appetite is high, so a calmer world and rate-cut hopes are a supportive mix. The market will keep taking cues from US data and the Fed alongside the geopolitical headlines.

For Indian investors, the swings come with a tax sting. India taxes crypto gains at an effective 34%, with a 1% TDS on transactions and no loss offset against other income, one of the harshest regimes globally, as our [crypto tax India 2026](/blog/crypto-tax-india-2026) guide explains. That makes timing and risk management especially costly to get wrong.

## What To Watch

The first thing to watch is risk sentiment. **Because crypto is moving with the broader risk-on, risk-off mood, the direction of equities and the geopolitics will heavily influence Bitcoin.**

The second is US data and the Fed. The rate-cut hopes underpinning crypto depend on soft inflation and jobs data, so upcoming prints could extend the recovery or stall it.

The third is whether Bitcoin can build on $64,000. Holding and pushing higher would confirm the recovery, while a slip back would suggest the bounce was fragile.

## Risks To Monitor

The clearest risk is a fresh geopolitical shock. A renewed escalation in the Gulf could quickly drag crypto back down alongside other risk assets.

A second risk is that rate-cut hopes fade. If US data comes in stronger, the macro tailwind behind crypto could weaken.

Bitcoin back near $64,000 shows how quickly crypto can recover once fear fades, but the week also showed how fast it can fall when fear strikes. In a market this sentiment-driven, the next move will depend on whether the calm holds or the Gulf conflict flares again.

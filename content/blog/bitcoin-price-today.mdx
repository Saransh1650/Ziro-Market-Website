Crypto has caught the market's fear again. **Bitcoin has slipped to about $62,550 as of July 14, 2026, down from around $64,000 late last week, with Ethereum near $1,788, as the escalating US-Iran war revived risk-off selling across markets.** After recovering the previous week, crypto has been pulled back down by the return of geopolitical stress.

The pattern is by now familiar. Crypto rises when calm returns and falls when fear spikes, and this week the fear came back with the war.

## What Is Happening

Bitcoin has given back its recent gains. **After recovering to about $64,000 the previous week, it has slipped to around $62,550 as the US-Iran war escalated**, with Ethereum easing to near $1,788. Both fell as investors turned cautious across risk assets.

The driver is risk sentiment. When the US bombed Iran for a third day and oil surged past $85, markets moved into risk-off mode, and crypto, as one of the highest-beta assets, fell alongside equities. **The rate-cut hopes that had supported crypto were overshadowed by the war fear**, at least for now.

The move keeps Bitcoin stuck in the $60,000 to $64,000 band it has traded all month, drifting up and down with the geopolitical mood rather than establishing a clear trend. It is a market waiting for the bigger picture to resolve.

## Why This Matters

The slide underlines crypto's link to the broader risk mood. **When Bitcoin falls with stocks on a war headline, it shows the market is trading on the same risk-off signal**, tying its near-term direction to the geopolitics and the dollar rather than a crypto-specific story.

The macro backdrop remains the key driver. Crypto tends to do best when liquidity is loose and risk appetite is high, so a tense, risk-averse environment is a headwind. The rate-cut hopes are still there in the background, but the war is dominating for now.

For Indian investors, the swings come with a tax sting. India taxes crypto gains at an effective 34%, with a 1% TDS on transactions and no loss offset against other income, one of the harshest regimes globally, as our [crypto tax India 2026](/blog/crypto-tax-india-2026) guide explains. That makes timing and risk management especially costly to get wrong.

## What To Watch

The first thing to watch is the geopolitics. **Because the slide is being driven by the US-Iran war, any de-escalation could quickly revive crypto, while further escalation would keep it under pressure.**

The second is US data and the Fed. The rate-cut hopes that could lift crypto depend on soft inflation and jobs data, so upcoming prints could reassert that tailwind even amid the noise.

The third is whether Bitcoin holds $60,000. Staying above the level would keep the recent range intact, while a slip below it would suggest the risk-off mood is deepening.

## Risks To Monitor

The clearest risk is a broad risk-off shock. A sharp escalation in the Gulf or a further spike in the dollar could pull crypto down alongside other risk assets.

A second risk is that rate-cut hopes fade. If US data comes in stronger, the macro tailwind behind crypto could weaken just as the geopolitical headwind blows.

The third is that the range breaks lower. Bitcoin has held $60,000 so far, but a decisive break below it could open the door to a deeper fall. This is general information, not investment advice.

Bitcoin back near $62,550 shows how quickly the war can flip crypto from recovery to retreat. In a market this sentiment-driven, the next move will depend on whether the Gulf conflict cools or escalates further, with the Fed a quieter voice in the background.

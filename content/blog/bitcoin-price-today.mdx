## What Is Happening

Bitcoin's June has been a slow grind lower. **After climbing above $71,000 earlier in the month, the price has slipped back into the $60,000 to $63,000 zone**, with June 24 marking a small recovery from a two-week low rather than a genuine breakout. At around $62,600, it is up modestly on the day but still well off its highs.

The drift reflects a broad cooling rather than a single shock. A firmer US dollar, a hawkish Federal Reserve signalling higher-for-longer rates, and steady profit-taking after the 2025 run have all weighed on risk assets. **Bitcoin now trades less like a world of its own and more like a high-beta version of the stock market**, rising and falling with the global mood.

Ethereum has had it worse. At about $1,648, it was down roughly 1% in a day and 7% over the week, underperforming Bitcoin in classic risk-off fashion, since altcoins typically fall harder when sentiment sours.

## Why This Matters

The gap to the all-time high tells the story of 2026. **At about $62,600, Bitcoin is trading roughly 50% below its October 2025 peak of $126,200**, a reminder of how far the rally has unwound. For investors who bought near the top, this has been a long and painful drawdown.

The macro backdrop is the key. As long as the dollar stays strong and the Fed leans hawkish, the cheap-money conditions that fuel crypto rallies remain absent. **Crypto tends to do best when liquidity is loose and rates are falling, and right now neither is true**, which is why bounces have struggled to hold.

For Indian investors, the swings come with a tax sting. India taxes crypto gains at an effective 34%, with a 1% TDS on transactions and no loss offset against other income, one of the harshest regimes globally. That makes timing and risk management especially costly to get wrong for Indian crypto holders.

## What To Watch

The first thing to watch is the US dollar and the Fed. **Because crypto now trades on macro, any softening in the dollar or a dovish shift from the Fed could spark a sharp relief rally**, just as their strength has capped prices all year.

The second is the $60,000 level. It has acted as a floor through June, so whether Bitcoin holds above it or breaks down will shape the near-term trend and could trigger forced selling from leveraged positions.

The third is whether ETF and institutional flows return. The 2025 rally was powered by inflows, so a pickup in buying from large funds would be an early sign that appetite is coming back.

## Risks To Monitor

The clearest risk is the macro backdrop staying tight. A persistently strong dollar and a hawkish Fed would keep a lid on crypto, and a clean break of $60,000 could accelerate selling.

A second risk is leverage. Crypto sell-offs often trigger cascading liquidations of leveraged positions, which can turn an orderly decline into a sharp crash within hours.

The third is sentiment fatigue. After months of grinding lower, even a stabilising market can struggle to attract fresh buyers, leaving prices stuck until a clear catalyst appears. This is general information, not investment advice.

Bitcoin near $62,600 sits in a holding pattern, too heavy to rally far while the dollar and the Fed stay firm, but finding support each time it tests $60,000. In a market this sentiment-driven, the next decisive move may be just one macro headline away.

Crypto's biggest question in the US is not a price, it is a rulebook. **The CLARITY Act, a proposed law that would finally set a clear regulatory framework for digital assets, is being pushed toward a July 4 milestone, with XRP watched as a key catalyst, even as prediction market Polymarket puts the odds of 2026 passage at just 48%.** For an industry long tangled in legal uncertainty, the bill is the event that matters most.

The stakes are about legitimacy as much as price. Clear rules would tell companies and investors exactly how digital assets are regulated in the world's largest financial market, removing a cloud that has hung over the industry for years.

## What Is Happening

The bill is the centrepiece of crypto's regulatory hopes. **The CLARITY Act aims to define which US agency oversees digital assets and how each is classified, as a security or a commodity, ending the ambiguity that has dogged the industry.** There has been a push to advance it, reportedly with a White House target around July 4.

XRP sits at the heart of the story. The token has long been the focal point of debates over how US law treats cryptocurrencies, so a clear market-structure law would remove a major question mark over it. Traders therefore treat the bill as a binary catalyst for XRP, an event whose yes-or-no outcome could sharply move the price.

But passage is far from certain. Polymarket, a prediction market, has trimmed the odds of the bill passing in 2026 to around 48%, with the Senate cloture math, the votes needed to break a filibuster and reach a final vote, still unresolved. So the July 4 push is a target, not a done deal.

## Why This Matters

Regulatory clarity is the missing piece for US crypto. **For years, the industry has operated without clear rules on who regulates what, which has slowed institutional adoption and kept some investors on the sidelines.** A law that settles those questions could unlock broader participation and reduce the legal risk that has shadowed crypto companies.

For the market, the bill is a sentiment driver. Progress toward clear, supportive rules tends to lift confidence across crypto, while setbacks disappoint. That is why a single legislative event can move not just XRP but the broader mood, alongside macro drivers like interest rates, as seen in Bitcoin's recent bounce covered on our [bitcoin price today](/blog/bitcoin-price-today) page.

<Callout type="risk" title="A bill is not a law">

A push toward a deadline is not the same as passage. With Senate votes uncertain and Polymarket odds near a coin-flip, the CLARITY Act could stall or slip into next year. Treating a binary catalyst as a sure thing is how investors get caught when the outcome disappoints.

</Callout>

## The India Angle

US crypto regulation reaches Indian investors through sentiment, not law. **Clearer, friendlier US rules tend to lift confidence and prices across the global crypto market, which can benefit Indian holders too**, since crypto trades as a global asset. A more legitimate US framework could also encourage broader institutional adoption worldwide.

What it does not change is India's own regime. India taxes crypto gains at an effective 34%, with a 1% TDS on transactions and no loss offset against other income, one of the strictest regimes globally. So even a supportive US law leaves the tax burden on Indian investors unchanged, a reminder that local rules matter as much as global ones.

## What To Watch

The first thing to watch is the Senate. **The bill's fate rests on whether it can clear the procedural hurdles, so the cloture vote math is the single most important signal for its chances.**

The second is XRP's reaction. As the token most tied to the outcome, XRP's price moves will reflect how the market is handicapping the bill in real time.

The third is the read-through to the broader market. If the bill advances, it could lift crypto sentiment generally; if it stalls, the disappointment could weigh, so the outcome matters beyond a single coin.

## Risks to Monitor

The clearest risk is that the bill fails or is delayed. With passage odds near 48%, a stall is a real possibility, and prices that have priced in success could reverse.

A second risk is the volatility of binary events. Catalysts with yes-or-no outcomes can whip prices sharply, so the run-up and aftermath can be turbulent regardless of the result.

The third, for Indian investors specifically, is that US progress does not ease India's heavy crypto tax, so the local cost of trading and holding remains high. This is general information, not investment advice.

The CLARITY Act is the closest crypto has come to the clear US rulebook it has long wanted, and XRP is the token carrying the most hope. But with Senate votes uncertain and passage odds near a coin-flip, the July 4 push is a test of whether the industry's biggest regulatory milestone is finally within reach, or still just out of it.
